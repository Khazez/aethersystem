Alatau elevation data

Distributed through Mapzen Terrain Tiles / AWS Open Data.
Accessed 2026-09-13. No endorsement of AetherSystem is implied.

Dataset: https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
Attribution: https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md
Format: https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/formats.md
Source tiles:
https://s3.amazonaws.com/elevation-tiles-prod/skadi/N43/N43E076.hgt.gz
https://s3.amazonaws.com/elevation-tiles-prod/skadi/N43/N43E077.hgt.gz

Modifications: crop to W76.68 E77.26 N43.215 S43.005; bilinear
resampling to 321 x 193 signed little-endian 16-bit metre heights.
Range 783..4545 metres above the reference geoid. Display subtracts an
850 m city datum and blends the grid's perimeter into the scene.
Vegetation, rock colours and snow cover are artistic, not satellite imagery.
Not for navigation, aircraft planning or engineering use.
